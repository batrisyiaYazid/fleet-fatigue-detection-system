# Problem Statement

Many road accidents  happen nowadays because of driver fatigue  that affect the driving performance. This may lead micro-sleeping behaviour among truck drivers, which are concerning because it may occur before the collisions. Kumagai et al.(2023) analyzed truck collision dashcam footage and found micro-sleep-related behaviours before the collision.
Then, this problem may affect the not only drivers themselves but other drivers as well that on the same roadHence, accidents involving heavy vehicles such as lorry or bus will give bigger impacts than other vehicles. As reported by Hassan(2025), Road Safety Activist Datuk Mohamad Hisham Shafe stated that lorries are heavy-duty vehicles that can cause significant impact in collision.
Last but not least, is the technical issue, which depends on a single feature to detect the fatigue signs. The system may miss other signs of fatigue and give unnecessary alarm. Hence, combining several indicators such as Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR), Mouth Aspect Ratio (MAR) and Percentage of Eye Closure (PERCLOS) can improve detection accuracy (Wijaya et al., 2026).
